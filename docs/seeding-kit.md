# JamWerk — Seeding kit (first scene: Geneva)

Goal of the first 4 weeks: **one city, one scene, real listings.** Liquidity in a
marketplace only starts when a musician opens the app and sees gigs that are
actually near them. Everything here is built to get to that state by hand.

## 1. Target & numbers

| What | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| City | Geneva + the French border towns (Annemasse, Saint-Julien-en-Genevois, Ferney-Voltaire, Saint-Genis-Pouilly, Gex, Divonne-les-Bains, Thonon-les-Bains, Évian, Valserhône/Bellegarde; Annecy is ~40 km) + Nyon / Lausanne | Home turf — you can meet people in person; dense wedding/corporate-event band scene, HEM jazz department, AMR jam culture, large international community |
| Scene | Cover / wedding / corporate-event bands first, then the AMR jazz & session scene | They book deps most often and pay known fees — exactly the paid-gig core |
| Week 1 | 20 bandleaders contacted, 8 profiles created, 3–5 real gigs posted | 3 visible gigs is the minimum for the board not to look dead |
| Week 2–4 | 30 musician profiles with alerts on, 1 completed gig with two-sided reviews | First "track record" on a public profile = the proof screenshot for the next outreach round |

Definition of a seeded city: ≥ 5 open listings and ≥ 25 musicians with alerts
enabled within 50 km. Only then open the next city.

## 2. Where to find the first 20 bandleaders

Work the list top-down; warm intros beat cold DMs 5:1.

1. **Your own network first** — every musician you know in Geneva / Vaud / the French border zone, and ask each for two names. You live here: coffee beats DMs.
2. **Wedding / corporate-event band agencies and band pages** — search "groupe de mariage Genève", "orchestre de mariage Genève", "cover band Genève", "groupe événementiel Vaud", "wedding band Geneva" (the international/expat scene books in English); band websites list the leader's contact. Leaders of 5–8-piece function bands replace sick members every month — corporate season (Sept–Dec) is when they need deps most.
3. **Jazz / session scene** — AMR (Sud des Alpes) jam sessions, Le Chat Noir in Carouge, HEM Genève jazz department students and alumni, ETM (École des musiques actuelles) — verify current session nights on their sites. Ask a session host to post one "looking to jam" listing and mention the app from the stage once.
4. **Music schools and rehearsal spaces** — Conservatoire populaire de musique, ETM, private schools and the rehearsal-room complexes (noticeboard flyer with a QR code to jamwerk.app; put one up in every room you rehearse in).
5. **Online boards where deps are already requested** — Facebook groups "musiciens Genève / Suisse romande", "musicien cherche groupe Genève", expat groups ("Geneva Expats" musicians threads), the Romandie band-finder forums. Answer existing "cherche bassiste pour samedi" posts with a JamWerk link and post the gig on their behalf if they agree.
6. **Cross-border (Grand Genève / Pays de Gex / Chablais)** — a large share of Geneva's working musicians live in France: Annemasse, Saint-Julien-en-Genevois, Ferney-Voltaire, Saint-Genis-Pouilly, Gex, Divonne-les-Bains, Thonon-les-Bains, Évian, Valserhône (Bellegarde), and Annecy. Sources on that side: the conservatoires and écoles de musique of Annemasse Agglo and Pays de Gex, the MJC music sections, "musiciens Annemasse / Pays de Gex / Haute-Savoie" Facebook groups, bars with live music in Annemasse and Thonon, and the Annecy jazz scene. The radius search already covers France (set the profile city to e.g. "Annemasse" and a 30 km radius — that includes all of Geneva). Say it explicitly: both sides see each other.

Track every contact in a simple sheet: name, band, instrument(s) needed most, channel, date contacted, status (contacted / profile / posted / booked).

### Reply when someone says "post it for me"
Ask exactly five things and post it yourself (see §5): instrument · date · city/venue · fee (CHF, whole gig) · two lines of description (repertoire, charts yes/no, rehearsal?). Then send them the listing link and the "My gigs" login so they can book the applicant.

## 5. Walkthrough — post the first gig in 2 minutes

UI labels in EN / DE / FR as they appear on jamwerk.app.

1. Open **jamwerk.app** → **Register / Registrieren / Créer un compte** (email, password, name). The confirmation mail can land in spam — the app says so.
2. Tab **Musician profile / Musikerprofil / Profil musicien** → set **City / Stadt / Ville** (this geocodes you; radius search and alerts depend on it) → **Save profile / Profil speichern / Enregistrer le profil**.
3. Tab **Post a gig / Anzeige veröffentlichen / Publier une annonce** → keep the *paid gig* kind → **Instrument needed**, **Date / Datum**, **City / Stadt / Ville**, **Fee (CHF, whole gig) / Gage (CHF, ganzer Gig) / Cachet (CHF, concert entier)**, genres, description (repertoire, charts?, soundcheck time) → **Post gig / Veröffentlichen / Publier**.
4. What happens: every musician with that instrument whose travel radius covers the venue gets an email + push ("Gig: bass in Geneva — CHF 300"). Applications arrive under **My gigs / Meine Gigs / Mes concerts** with name, rating and gigs played — contact details are shared only after you **Book**.
5. After the gig: mark it completed → both sides review. The review shows on the musician's public page (`jamwerk.app/m/handle`) — screenshot it, that is your next outreach material.

### Walkthrough — a dep's first 2 minutes
Register → profile (instruments, **Experience level / Erfahrungsstufe / Niveau**, city, **Travel radius (km)**, up to 5 demo links) → tap the **bell / Alerts** in the header and allow notifications (on iPhone: add to home screen first). Done — they will be notified of every matching gig.

## 6. Objections you will hear

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "I have my WhatsApp group for that." | Keep it. JamWerk is for the Saturday when the group stays silent — and it reaches players you don't know yet, with ratings. |
| "Public fee? Awkward." | Public fee is the filter: nobody applies who wouldn't play for it, no haggling by DM. You set the number. |
| "There's nothing in the app." | True today — that's why I'm asking you. Your listing is the first one the next 20 people see. |
| "Half my deps live in France." | Good — the radius search covers the French side; they can register with their French town and get alerts exactly like Swiss players. |
| "I'm in Annemasse — do Geneva gigs show up?" | Yes: a 30 km radius from Annemasse covers all of Geneva and Nyon. Fees are shown in CHF (that's how Geneva gigs pay anyway). |
| "Data / privacy?" | Email is never shown; contact only after booking; Swiss product, no ads, no selling data. |
| "Is it free?" | Yes. Monetization, if ever, will be on the paid-gig side and announced up front — never on jams or profiles. |

## 7. Weekly cadence & metrics

Mon: 10 new contacts. Wed: follow-ups. Fri: post/refresh gigs, screenshot any new review, send it to 5 contacts. Sun: pull the numbers.

Numbers to pull each week (D1 `jamwerk-db`; the agent can run these via the D1 MCP):

```sql
SELECT count(*) AS musicians, sum(confirmed) AS confirmed FROM users;
SELECT count(*) AS with_alerts FROM push_subscriptions;
SELECT kind, status, count(*) FROM gigs GROUP BY kind, status;
SELECT count(*) AS applications FROM gig_applications;
SELECT count(*) AS reviews FROM gig_reviews;
```

Leading indicator: **applications per open gig within 48 h**. ≥ 2 means the
city works; < 1 after three gigs means the dep side is too thin — switch the
outreach mix to 80 % musicians for a week.

Being local: every Friday, go to one session night with the app open on your
phone and onboard two people in person. One in-person profile is worth ten
DMs at this stage.
